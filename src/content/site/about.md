Orges Kokoshari is a CG generalist and look-dev artist based in New York City. Across twenty years he has built hero assets end to end, from sculpt and model through texturing, look-dev, lighting, and animation, for feature film, episodic television, and advertising.

His film credits include Extraction II, No One Will Save You, The SpongeBob Movie: Search for SquarePants, Contraband, and The Judge. On television he has worked on Apple TV's Star City, Netflix's One Piece, Class of '09, and Naomi, built 3D for the main title sequence of Netflix's Godless, and contributed character and asset work to American Horror Story.

His commercial work spans Google's mobile and wearable launches, Super Bowl spots with Larry David and Mr. Peanut, campaigns for Honda, Ford, Nissan, AT&T, Nike, and Samsung, game trailers for God of War and Splinter Cell: Blacklist, and music videos for Coldplay and Kanye West. That work has run through Framestore, Method Studios, FuseFX, Preymaker, Imaginary Forces, The Mill, Buck, MPC, and R/GA. He currently works as a Technical Artist at Meta and holds a BFA in Computer Graphics from Pratt Institute.

His personal work runs from fine art to CG concept development to 3D printing. These projects keep the creative side fed, and they're where emerging tools and pipeline approaches get tested first, which is often how they find their way into client work.
